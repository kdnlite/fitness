### Full description (4000)

KDN Fitness is your personal fitness coach for everyday Indian life. Tell KDN a few things about you and it builds a complete plan: what to eat, how to train, how many steps to walk and how fast you can safely reach your goal.

DIET MADE FROM FOOD YOU ALREADY EAT
• A 7-day meal plan built from Indian home food: poha, dal, roti, chilla, paneer, eggs, chicken and more
• Vegetarian, vegan and non-vegetarian plans
• Calories and protein for every meal, with a plan that fits your food budget
• A weekly grocery list, sorted by aisle
• Snap what you ate: take a photo and KDN estimates the foods for you

WORKOUTS FOR HOME OR THE GYM
• Day-by-day workouts for your level, your place and the days you can train
• Hundreds of exercises with clear start and end photos and short demo videos
• Guided workout mode: one exercise at a time with sets and rest timers

STEPS, PROGRESS AND STREAKS
• Step counter with a daily ring, streaks and badges
• Weekly weight check-ins and a progress chart that shows the road to your goal
• A daily score that keeps you moving

KDN AI
• Ask questions or log by voice: "my weight is 72 kg", "I ate two rotis and dal"

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your body details, meals, workouts and steps stay on your phone
• No ads, no tracking

KDN gives estimates from standard formulas. It is not medical advice. If you have a health condition, are pregnant or are under 18, talk to a doctor or dietitian before changing your diet or exercise.

## App content answers

- **Ads:** No ads.
- **App access:** Some features need a KDN plan. Give reviewers the test coupon in "App access" (instructions: open the app, tap Get started, enter the coupon on the plans screen).
- **Content rating (IARC):** Category "Utility, productivity, communication or other" → no violence, no sexual content, no profanity, no drugs, no gambling, no user-to-user chat. Expected rating: Everyone / 3+.
- **Target audience:** 18 and over (keeps the app out of the Families program).
- **News app:** No. **Government app:** No. **Financial features:** No.
- **Health apps declaration:** Fitness, nutrition and diet; activity and step tracking. Not a medical device.
- **Health Connect:** reads Steps only (READ_STEPS). Use: show daily steps, distance, calories, streaks inside the app. Data stays on the phone. Privacy policy section 4a explains it.

## Data safety form

- Does the app collect or share user data? **Yes** (collected, not shared).
- Is all data encrypted in transit? **Yes** (HTTPS).
- Can users ask for their data to be deleted? **Yes** (email support@kdnfitness.com; local data is deleted by logging out or uninstalling).
- Data types collected (sent off the phone):
  - **Personal info → Email address, Name:** collected when buying a plan. Purpose: account management, app functionality, developer communications. Not optional for paid plans.
  - **Financial info → Purchase history:** subscription records. Purpose: account management. (Payments are processed by Razorpay.)
  - **Photos:** only when the user uses "snap what you ate"; processed to find foods and not stored. Mark as collected, **processed ephemerally**, optional, purpose app functionality.
  - **App activity → Other actions:** none.
- Data that stays only on the device (body details, meals, workouts, steps, period tracker) is **not collected** under Play's definition, so it is not declared.

## Testing track (required for a new personal account)

Google requires a closed test with at least **12 testers for 14 days in a row** before the app can go to Production. Testers need Gmail addresses and must opt in from the test link and keep the app installed.
