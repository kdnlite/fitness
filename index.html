    About KDN

    KDN Fitness Tracker turns a few body measurements and your food and goal choices into a simple daily plan.

    How are calories worked out?Resting energy comes from the Mifflin-St Jeor formula. It is multiplied by 1.2 for normal daily life, and the net calories of your own planned workouts (MET value × body weight × time, minus the resting part) are added. That is what you burn. About 7,700 kcal is 1 kg of body weight, so each 1,100 kcal a day below or above that moves you about 1 kg a week.
    How are protein, carbs and fat set?Protein is 1.8 g per kg when losing, 1.8 g when gaining and 1.4 g when staying stable, counted on your weight (or an adjusted weight if your BMI is 30 or more) and capped at 40% of calories. Fat is 25% of calories, never below 0.6 g per kg. Carbs fill the rest. Fibre is 14 g per 1,000 kcal.
    How fast can I go?The recommended pace is about 0.75% of body weight a week for losing and 0.2 to 0.35 kg for gaining. You can choose any faster time up to 1 kg a week. Faster than the comfortable range gets a clear warning. More than 1 kg a week is refused because it is not practical or safe. Calories never go below 1,200 kcal (women), 1,500 kcal (men) or 1,350 kcal (other).
    Will this guarantee my result?No plan can promise that. The numbers come from standard formulas, which fit most people within a few percent, and followed closely they point you to your goal. Because bodies differ, the Progress tab uses your weekly weigh-ins to compare your real trend with the plan and suggests a small calorie change (up to 400 kcal) when needed.
    How are the dishes and workouts chosen?Each dish has its nutrition added up from ingredient tables, then its portion is sized so your day lands close to your calories and protein. Your workouts follow your goal, where you train, your level and your days a week: 4, 5 or 6 exercises per session and 2, 3 or 4 sets.
    Are the pictures and videos real?No. Dish and exercise pictures are drawn illustrations. The exercise demos and the home-page scene are animated drawings of original male and female characters in KDN tees. They are not photos or AI-generated video. Each exercise also links to real videos on YouTube.
    Where is my data saved?Only in this browser on this device (local storage). Nothing is uploaded. You stay logged in on this device until you choose Log out from the menu, which removes it.
    Is this medical advice?No. If you have a health condition, are pregnant or are under 18, talk to a doctor or dietitian first.
