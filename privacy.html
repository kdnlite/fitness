KDN Fitness

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026. This policy covers the KDN Fitness website (kdnfitness.com) and the KDN Fitness Android app.

In short: your body details, plan, steps, medicines, medical reports and period data are saved only on your own phone. They are not uploaded to us. We only keep your email, name and subscription details when you buy a plan, so we can give you access and help you.

1. Data that stays on your device

KDN works on your device. The following are stored only in the app or browser on your phone (local storage and IndexedDB). We do not receive, see or sell them:

  • Profile and plan: name, age, gender, height, weight, goal, diet, activity and workout choices.
  • Tracking: meals, workouts, steps, weight check-ins and progress.
  • My health (medicines and medical records are on the website only; the Android app has the period tracker): medicines and dose history, your medical ID (blood group, allergies, conditions, emergency contact, doctor), medical records and the photos and PDF files you add, and period-tracker data (dates, flow, symptoms, mood, notes).
  • Family plan members and Gym Manager data (members, fees, staff, attendance) that you enter.

This data is removed when you log out in KDN, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. You can make a backup file from My health and keep it yourself. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app, so keep your phone locked.

2. Data we receive

  • When you buy a plan: your email address, your name (if you give it), the plan you chose, the coupon you used and the payment reference. Payments are handled by Razorpay. We never see your card, UPI PIN or bank login. We store your KDN ID and the date your plan is valid until, and email them to you.
  • When you write to support@kdnfitness.com: your email address and your message, so we can reply.
  • Food photos (only when you use "snap what you ate"): the photo is made smaller on your phone and sent to kdnfitness.com, where Cloudflare Workers AI looks at it and returns the foods it sees. The photo is not saved by us or used for anything else. To keep a daily limit we count photos per day using a scrambled (hashed) form of your internet address, which we delete after a few days.
  • Prices and announcements: the website and the app load the current plan prices from kdnfitness.com. This request does not include any of your personal data.

Visit counts (website only): to see how many people visit and make a plan, the website adds 1 to a daily counter when you open it, start the questions, make your first plan or open the plans page. Only the date and the counter are stored: no IP address, cookie, name or any identifier. The Android app does not send these counts.

We do not show ads. We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or rent your data.

3. Service providers

  • Cloudflare hosts kdnfitness.com and our small database of subscriptions and support emails, and runs the AI that reads food photos (Workers AI).
  • Razorpay processes payments.
  • Resend sends our emails (for example your KDN ID and plan validity).
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts used by the website and the app.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

4. App permissions

  • Notifications: in the app, to remind you about your expected period, even when the app is closed. On the website, also for medicine doses. You can turn them off in your phone or browser settings.
  • Camera (website only): only when you choose "Take photo" to add a medical report. The photo is saved on your device. The Android app does not ask for the camera.
  • Microphone (only when you tap the mic): to hear what you say to KDN AI or "say what you ate". In the Android app, speech is turned into text by your phone's speech service; KDN does not record or keep the audio.
  • Health Connect (Android app, only if you tap Connect): to read your daily step count. See section 4a.
  • Motion sensor: to count your steps while KDN is open. Step counts stay on your device.

4a. Health Connect

If you choose "Connect" on the Steps screen of the Android app, KDN asks Health Connect for permission to read your step count. KDN does not ask for, read or write any other Health Connect data.

  • Step counts are used only to show your daily steps, distance, calories, score, streak, badges and weekly report inside KDN.
  • They are saved only on your phone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, not sold, and not used for advertising.
  • You can remove the permission any time in Health Connect settings. Logging out of KDN or uninstalling the app deletes the copy KDN keeps.

KDN's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your device: delete it any time by logging out, clearing the app's data or uninstalling. Data we hold (email, subscription and payment records, support emails): we keep it while your plan is active and as long as needed for accounts and tax law. To delete it, email support@kdnfitness.com from the address you used. We reply within 30 days.

6. Security

Connections to kdnfitness.com use HTTPS. Our admin panel is protected by a password and a login lock. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we hold to a minimum.

7. Health information

KDN gives estimates from standard formulas. It is not medical advice and does not replace a doctor. Period predictions are estimates and are not a method of birth control.

8. Children

KDN is meant for people aged 13 and older. Anyone under 18 should use it with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
